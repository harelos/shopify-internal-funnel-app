Attribution safeguards

Verified tier-1 revenue only: an order must arrive from the Shopify server/webhook path and join to the signed attribution chain. Duplicate delivery is idempotent; missing links remain unattributed instead of guessed.

Refund/cancellation reconciliation reduces net revenue instead of permanently claiming original gross revenue.
