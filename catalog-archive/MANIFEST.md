Exported 2026-09-14 from jacobfelipe.myshopify.com (TigerBrandsGlobal).

These came from Shopify Collective. They sit at 0 inventory because Collective requires the
retailer store to use the same country and currency as the supplier store. Suppliers are US/USD;
this store is ILS. Restoring them requires either switching the store to USD or re-sourcing the
same products from a supplier that ships to Israel.

## Restoring

1. Shopify > Products > Import, upload `collective-catalog-shopify-import.csv`.
   This rebuilds titles, Hebrew descriptions, images, SEO, options, prices and compare-at prices.
2. Re-import the products through the Collective app to re-establish live inventory and supplier
   fulfilment. The CSV restores the content work, not the supplier link.
3. Remove the matching rows from `redirects.csv` in Shopify > Navigation > URL Redirects, so the
   product URLs resolve to the products again instead of the hair collection.

## About the images

`Image Src` points at this store's own Shopify CDN. Those files live in Shopify > Content > Files and
survive archiving, so the CSV re-imports cleanly. They would only be at risk if the products were
hard-deleted and the files purged, which is why the products are being **archived, not deleted**.
If you ever want a fully offline copy, the image URLs in `collective-catalog.json` can be downloaded
in bulk, but they are deliberately not committed here to keep the repo free of binaries.
